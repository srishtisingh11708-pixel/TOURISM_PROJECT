Which e-Visa is right for you?

e-Tourist Visa

Designed for sightseeing, casual visits to friends or family, and short recreational trips. This is the most common e-Visa issued to travelers from 160+ countries.

  • Validity options: 30 days, 1 year, or 5 years
  • Double entry permitted on the 30-day version
  • Apply 4–120 days before your arrival date
  • Cannot be extended or converted to another visa type

e-Business Visa

For attending trade meetings, setting up a business venture, recruiting, or delivering lectures under a government-recognized program.

  • Valid for up to 1 year, multiple entries
  • Requires a business invitation or trade proof
  • Can be clubbed with tourist activities
  • Processing typically takes 3–4 working days

e-Medical Visa

For patients seeking treatment at recognized Indian hospitals, including Ayurveda, homeopathy, and other traditional systems of medicine.

  • Triple entry allowed, valid up to 60 days
  • One companion may apply for an e-Medical Attendant visa
  • Requires a letter from the treating hospital
  • Extendable locally at the hospital's district FRRO

e-Conference Visa

For delegates attending a conference, seminar, or workshop hosted by a government ministry, department, or recognized public sector body.

  • Single entry, valid for the conference duration
  • Requires official conference invitation letter
  • Can only be clubbed with tourist-visa activities
  • Apply at least 4 days before the conference date

The Process

Four steps to your ETA

01

Apply Online

Fill the form on the official portal with passport and personal details. Takes about 10–15 minutes.

02

Upload Documents

Submit a digital passport photo and your passport's data page as clear scans (JPG/PDF).

03

Pay & Wait

Complete the processing fee payment; approval (ETA) usually arrives within 24–72 hours by email.

04

Travel & Land

Carry a printout of your ETA — biometrics are captured on arrival at any designated airport or seaport.

Don't Get Rejected

Documents you'll actually need

📘
Valid Passport

At least 6 months validity from arrival date, with 2 blank pages for stamping.

🖼️
Passport-Style Photo

Recent, plain white background — different from your passport photo.

🎫
Return Ticket Proof

Confirmed onward or return travel booking, matching your e-Visa dates.

🏨
Accommodation Proof

Hotel booking confirmation or an invitation letter if staying with family.
