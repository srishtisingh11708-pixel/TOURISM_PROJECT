By Road

Route Notes

  • From the capital, take NH-44 north and follow signs toward the valley — the road is well-paved for most of the stretch.
  • Stop at Ridgeview Point (2.5 hrs in) for the best overlook photo of the whole trip.
  • Self-drive, taxis, and overnight buses are all available; taxis can be booked at the stand outside any major station.
  • Fuel stations thin out after the halfway mark — top up at Bantwal junction.
Distance310 km
Drive time6h 30m

Road conditionGood, hilly last 40 km

Tip: leave before 6 AM to clear the ghat section before the afternoon mist rolls in.

By Train

Route Notes

  • Board the Valley Express from Central Station — reserve a window seat on the left for the best views after Kundapur.
  • Three departures daily: early morning, early afternoon, and a scenic sunset run recommended for first-time visitors.
  • The nearest station to town is a 15-minute auto ride from the main square, autos wait right outside the exit.
  • Book at least a week ahead in peak season — the sunset run sells out first.
Duration4h 45m
Frequency3 trains / day
Best forScenic, relaxed travel
Nearest stationCentral Station

Tip: the sunset departure crosses the Kundapur bridge right at golden hour — worth planning around.

By Flight

Route Notes

  • Two daily flights connect from the capital — morning and evening — landing at the regional airport just 12 km from town.
  • Prepaid taxis and hotel shuttles are available right outside arrivals.
  • The airport is small — arrive 60 minutes before departure, no need for the usual buffer.
  • Fares are lowest booked 3+ weeks ahead; the evening flight tends to run cheaper.

Tip: window seats on the left of the evening flight catch the valley lighting up at dusk.
